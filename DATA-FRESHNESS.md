## How to read this

- **GREEN** — data updated within 2 days.
- **AMBER** — stale 3-6 days, or rows/SKUs dropped sharply vs the trailing median.
- **RED** — no update for > 6 days, or zero rows. **Actionable RED gates the cron (nonzero exit).** Known-dead platforms are RED but excluded from the gate.
- Freshness uses the **freshest** signal across `baselines/<p>.json` AND `data/<p>/history.csv`, so a lagging baseline on a still-flowing platform does not raise a false alarm.
